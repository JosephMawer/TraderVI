# ADR-0125 — Reinvest reconciled strategy sale proceeds

Accepted November 3, 2026. The operator wants the ongoing Real strategy to rotate its
approved capital into later positions and reinvest realized profits. The prior cumulative
ceiling could exhaust purchase authority while verified cash remained available.

For an explicitly reviewed ongoing agreement, remaining purchase authority is the original
approved capital less committed purchase costs and pending purchase reservations, plus net
proceeds of fully reconciled sales owned by this agreement. Include qualifying sales since
the agreement began when adopting the terms. A loss returns less capital. Fees reduce the
amount returned. Use each original order's cumulative receipt once, including after restart.
An unresolved, partial or conflicted sale cannot fund a new buy.

Before a purchase, reconcile the no-transfer cash anchor, owned orders and actual holdings.
Limit the order by available CAD cash, buying power, the strategy's whole-share sizing and
all existing risk, session, quote and protection checks. Outside deposits confer no new
authority. Unexplained cash movement blocks new buying. The existing agreement requires a
pause before outside activity because matching balances cannot prove offsetting transfers
never occurred.

The desktop shows the terms before approval. Existing ongoing accounts adopt them through
the stopped-host review, immutable successor settings and separate host enable. Bind the
terms to the review identity and verify the saved mode against the compact account before
execution. Earlier approvals retain their historical meaning. This supersedes the
non-replenishing rule in [ADR-0085](0085-portfolio-wide-ongoing-tfsa-management.md) and
[ADR-0109](0109-explicit-ongoing-delphi-live-management.md) only for opted-in agreements.

# Brief title

**Date:** YYYY-MM-DD · **Status:** Draft / Proposed / Accepted / Completed

> [!summary] Executive summary
> State the outcome or proposed decision in two sentences. Explain why it matters. Keep any limitation that changes the decision visible here.

## At a glance

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| What problem are we solving? | Immediate problem and the larger goal it serves. |
| What do we know? | The strongest verified result, with units and comparison. |
| What should happen next? | A concrete recommendation and its expected benefit. |

## Next decision

State the recommended action, its tradeoff, and any decision the reader needs to make. Use a short numbered list only when several priorities genuinely compete.

## Supporting detail

> [!note]- Evidence and reasoning
> Add source links, observations and alternatives here. Distinguish findings from hypotheses.

> [!note]- Validation and limitations
> Describe what was verified, what remains uncertain and the limits of the conclusion.

> [!note]- Implementation and recovery
> For a design decision, retain contracts, affected components and recovery details here. Omit this section when it does not apply.
